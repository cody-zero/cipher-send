# Secure Secret Sharing

The CipherSend app runs entirely in the browser, in a single page that moves through these steps.

## 1. Share — Host view

Send this link or QR code to the person you're sharing with.

A QR code and the session link are generated here, with a countdown bar and a "Connecting..." status.

> Scan with your phone's built-in camera app only — third-party QR scanner apps are known to transmit scanned links to their own servers.

[Back to Home](/)

## 2. Join — Guest view

The person who invited you is expecting you. Confirming you're here with a click keeps automated link previews from silently taking the session before you arrive.

- **Join secure session** — click to connect. Status then reads "Connecting to secure session...".

[Back to Home](/)

## 3. Verify — Host + Guest view

Read this phrase to each other over your call, chat, or in person. If it matches on both screens, click Approve.

> This confirms you're connected to the right person, not an imposter.

- **Approve** — enabled once the shared phrase is shown. Status reads "Waiting for peer approval..." until the other side approves.

[Back to Home](/)

## 4. Chat

The connected session between Host and Guest. The header menu links to Home, How It Works, Why CipherSend, Use Cases, Security, Privacy, and FAQ.

- Input box: *Type your secret... (Ctrl + Enter to send)* — up to 5000 characters, with a live counter.
- **Send** button, plus a **clipboard-delete** button with a "Content copied" tip and clear countdown.
- If the peer leaves: *Peer disconnected — this session will end shortly.*

## If the app can't run here

**Open in your browser**

Your current browser or app can't run the security checks CipherSend depends on.

- **Copy link** — copy the session link and open it in your regular browser.

[Back to Home](/)
